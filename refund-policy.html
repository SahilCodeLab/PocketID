Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

This page outlines the cancellation policy for PocketID services during the early access phase.

Free Early Access

PocketID is currently in an early access phase and is provided to users **free of charge**. No payments are currently required to use the core features of the app.

1. Cancellation of Account

You can discontinue the use of PocketID at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, please use the "Delete Account" option within the app settings. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered as we do not store backups of your private documents.

2. Refund Policy

Since PocketID is currently free to use, no refund policy is applicable at this stage. Should we introduce paid features in the future, a comprehensive refund policy will be updated here.

3. Subscription Changes

In the event that you have registered for early access updates, you can unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in our emails.
