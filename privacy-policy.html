Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 12, 2026

Welcome to PocketID. Your privacy and the security of your data are our top priorities. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Data Sovereignty (Offline First)

PocketID is a "Local-First" application. This means we do NOT store your personal documents, ID cards, notes, or certificates on our servers. All your sensitive data is stored locally on your device's internal storage using industry-standard encryption. We have no access to your personal files.

2. Information We Collect

  • No Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account with your name or email.
  • Device Information: We collect your unique Device ID solely to manage your "Pro" license and ensure your subscription works on your device.
  • Camera Access: Used only when you choose to scan a physical document. We do not record or store anything beyond the scan you save.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide a better experience, we use:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying ads. AdMob may use device identifiers to show relevant ads.
  • Razorpay: To process payments for the "Pro" version. We do not store your payment details; they are handled securely by Razorpay.

4. Data Backup

If you use the 'Export Backup' feature, a file is created on your phone. You are responsible for keeping this file safe. If you delete the app or lose your phone without a backup, your data cannot be recovered by us.
