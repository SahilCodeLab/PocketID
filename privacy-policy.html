Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

Welcome to PocketID ("we," "our," or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how SahilCodeLab collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the PocketID mobile application and website.

Key Privacy Commitment: PocketID is built with a "Privacy-First" architecture. Your sensitive documents are stored locally on your device or in your private encrypted cloud storage. We do not have access to your private documents.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services effectively, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, and phone number when you register for early access or Pro membership.
  • Document Metadata: Information about the types of cards or documents you store (e.g., "Aadhaar Card" or "License") to help organize your vault. Note: The actual content of the documents is encrypted.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our app, such as features used and time spent, collected via internal analytics to improve user experience.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the functionality of PocketID.
  • To issue authentic digital certificates upon successful registration.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To send you important updates regarding our services.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Local Encryption: Your documents are encrypted using AES-256 encryption before being stored on your device.
  • No Server-Side Document Storage: We do not store your identity documents on our central servers.
  • Secure Transmission: Any data transmitted between the app and our servers is protected using SSL/TLS encryption.

4. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and individuals due to the following reasons:

  • AdMob (Google): We may use AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may collect data to show relevant ads.
  • Google Drive/iCloud: If you choose to enable cloud backup, your data is stored in your private storage.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
