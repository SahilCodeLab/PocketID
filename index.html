Knowledge

Common Questions

Does PocketID collect my personal data? +
Absolutely not. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number. Only your Device ID is used for ad management and license synchronization.
Where are my documents stored? +
Your documents are stored in an encrypted vault on your phone's internal storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to export a backup.
How do I get the Pro features? +
You can earn in-app coins by watching advertisements or through daily rewards. These coins can be used to unlock Pro features.
What if I lose my phone? +
Since we don't store your data on our servers, we cannot recover it for you. We highly recommend using the 'Export Backup' tool regularly.
